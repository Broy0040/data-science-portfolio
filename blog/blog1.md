# About Me
Hi! I’m Brandon Roy, and I'm a Data Science and Mathematics for Business student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte with a 4.0 GPA. My path in data science is built on a passion for using mathematical modeling, statistics, and analytics to solve practical real-world problems. I love taking complex datasets and translating them into clear, actionable insights that help organizations make better business decisions.

On the technical side, I work primarily with Python, Java, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Excel to explore data and build visualizations. But for me, data science isn't just about writing code or crunching numbers in an isolated environment. Communication and teamwork are just as important as the quantitative side of the field.

My experience outside the classroom has really shaped how I work with others. For the past several summers, I’ve worked as a Camp Counselor for the City of Concord, supervising energetic groups of 30 to 60 kids across daily activities and offsite trips. Managing large groups under fast-paced conditions taught me how to communicate clearly, adapt quickly when things don't go according to plan, and step up as a leader. Those everyday soft skills directly translate into how I approach cross-functional teamwork, problem-solving, and analytical projects today.

Looking ahead, I am actively seeking analytics and data science internship opportunities in the Charlotte, NC area as well as remote positions. I am excited to apply my skills to hands-on data projects, collaborate with a strong team, and keep growing as a professional.
